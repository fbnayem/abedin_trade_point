Do you accept bulk enquiries from businesses and institutions?

Yes. Send the products, quantities and specifications you need, together with your organisation's delivery requirements.

Can I request several different items together?

Yes. Add products to your quote list or upload one purchase list containing multiple items.

What is the minimum order quantity?

Minimum quantities depend on the product, specification and supply arrangement. Any applicable minimum will be confirmed in the quotation.

Why are prices shown as “Price on quotation”?

Pricing can depend on quantity, specifications, branding, availability and delivery requirements. A quotation is prepared after the details are reviewed.

Can I request products that are not listed?

Yes. Send the product name, quantity and specification. We will review whether the requirement can be sourced.

Can products include our logo or custom design?

You can include branding in your enquiry. Available methods, artwork requirements and charges will be confirmed for the proposed product.

Can I see a sample before ordering?

Ask for a sample in your enquiry. Availability, charges and timing depend on the product and supply arrangement.

Where can you deliver, and how long will it take?

Share your delivery location and required date. Coverage, charges and timing will be confirmed for the proposed order.

What payment terms apply?

The written quotation will state the applicable payment terms and any deposit required before the order proceeds.

Do I need to create an account?

No. You can submit a quotation request as a guest using the website form.
